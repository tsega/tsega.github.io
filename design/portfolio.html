About

I'm Tsega — a senior software architect who's spent 20+ years designing systems: custom CMS platforms, cloud-native infrastructure, and the integrations that hold them together. My path ran through seven years as CTO of Cybersoft, senior architecture consulting for Symphony3 in Melbourne, and full-stack delivery for global brands — L'Oréal, Bayer, and the UK Hydrographic Office among them.

Drupal is one system I know deeply — it's where a lot of that architecture work has lived. But the toolkit runs wider: PHP and Symfony, Node.js, Vue and React, Ruby on Rails, C# — backed by Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines I've built from scratch. Two Acquia certifications, and a growing interest in Fintech, applied AI, and graph databases.

Outside client work, I organize and mentor. I lead Amharic translation for Drupal, founded and run the Ethiopian Drupal community, and have trained developers directly — from a Women in Tech bootcamp in Addis Ababa to onboarding new engineers at the Orange Innovation Hub in Dakar.

Amharic — native English — fluent Open to: Architecture · Fintech · AI · Community
